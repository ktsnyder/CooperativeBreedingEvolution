This file contains data on parental cooperation in birds, predictor variables used in the analyses, and references used to collate the data. For details, see the accompanying publication.

It is a csv file, where missing data are blanks.

For questions contact Vladimír Remeš (email address in the accompanying publication).

Column headings:

Species_name    Name of the  bird species, follows nomenclature used in Jetz et al. (2012) Nature 491(7424):444–448
Cooperation Parental cooperation as defined in the accompanying paper
Male_care   Relative male care as defined in the accompanying paper
Chick_dev   Chick developmental mode, 1 = altricial, 2 = precocial
EPP Extra-pair paternity, % of nests with at least 1 EPP young
EPY Extra-pair paternity, % of EPP young in the population
Body_mass   Adult body mass of the species (grams)
SDI Sexual size dimorphism expressed as Sexual Dimorphism Index (definition in the accompanying paper)
ASR Adult sex ratio, proportion of males in the population
Refs_Care_Dev   References for parental care (Cooperation and Male_care) and chick developmental mode (Chick_dev)
Refs_Paternity  References for extra-pair paternity (EPP and EPY)
Refs_ASR    References for adult sex ratio (ASR)
Refs_Body_mass  References for adult body mass (Body_mass) and sexual size dimorphism (SDI)
