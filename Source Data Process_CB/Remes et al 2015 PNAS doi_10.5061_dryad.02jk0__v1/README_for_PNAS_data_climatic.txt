This file contains data on climatic variables used in analyses of parental cooperation in birds. These are climatic variables for the whole ranges of the species during the breeding season. Detailed methods how the variables were calculated and what data sources were used during these calculations are available in the Methods of the accompanying publication.

It is a csv file, where missing data are blanks.

For questions contact Vladimír Remeš (email address in the accompanying publication).

Column headings:

Species_name    Name of the  bird species, follows nomenclature used in Jetz et al. (2012) Nature 491(7424):444–448
season.temp.mean    Mean temperature
season.temp.sd  Variability of temperature across years
season.temp.sd.within   Variability of temperature within the year
season.logprec.mean Mean rainfall
season.logprec.sd   Variability of rainfall across years
season.logprec.sd.within    Variability of rainfall within the year
PC.temperature  Principal Component Analysis axis no 1 run on the climatic variables (factor loadings in the accompanying paper)
PC.rainfall Principal Component Analysis axis no 2 run on the climatic variables (factor loadings in the accompanying paper)
